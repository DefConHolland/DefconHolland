Yesterday was the second meetup of the year for DefCon Holland! As usual, the atmosphere was great, there were fun people, and we had interesting speakers.

![crowd](img/2nd_meetup_2026_crowd.jpg)

This time, the meetup took place in our capital city at the cozy Cafe Tapmarin.

The first talk was a lightning talk, presented by Eden.
![eden](img/2nd_meetup_2026_eden.jpg)

The title of her talk was “Supabase Shenanigans: Hacking Websites With The Same Misconfiguration”.  
The full writeup can be found here: [https://skelli.win/posts/supabase-shenanigans/](https://skelli.win/posts/supabase-shenanigans/)

The second speaker was “Cookie Engineer”.
![cookieengineer](img/2nd_meetup_2026_cookieengineer.JPG)

His talk was about “Agentic Environments - what works and what doesn’t”.

Both speakers explained these interesting topics with a good dose of humor.

The rest of the evening was for socializing and networking. Will we see you (again) at the next one? I hear it’s going to be a BBQ… See you there!
